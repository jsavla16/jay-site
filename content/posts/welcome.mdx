This site started as a build log: notes written when something breaks and
gets fixed, kept in `NOTES.md`, expanded into posts later.

Two projects feed it — this site itself, and [Umojah Sound
System](https://umojahsoundsystem.com), a reggae dub sound system I
co-founded in Kenya. Both are built on Next.js and deployed on Vercel.

The habit: when something breaks, write down what happened, what it looked
like, what it actually was, and what it cost. Five lines is enough at the
time — the polished version comes later.
